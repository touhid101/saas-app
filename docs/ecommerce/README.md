# docs/ecommerce — Global-customer, multi-branch B2B e-commerce on the Friends Pharma ERP

Phase 0 (analysis) is complete. **Phase 13 (loyalty points) is implemented**: V111 adds `ECOM_POINT_RULE` / `_BRANCH` / `ECOM_POINT_LEDGER` and a *Loyalty points* tab beside the coupons on **MOD54 · E-Commerce Promotions** — nothing is seeded and there is no default exchange rate, because what a point is worth is the pharmacy's money and its decision; points are credited when an order **arrives** (never when it is placed, and never twice, whether the stage moves on the board or in Sales Activity), spent at checkout as a request the server caps against the balance, the rule's ceiling and the order, returned by an `ADJUST` row when an order is cancelled, and landing on the invoice through the same exact-amount discount Phase 12 built; the balance is the ledger's sum, staff move one by hand only with a reason and only with MOD54 APPROVE, and the customer reads the same statement under Account → Points (`16 §4`). **Phase 12 (coupons) is implemented**: V110 adds `ECOM_COUPON` / `_BRANCH` / `_USAGE` and the **MOD54 · E-Commerce Promotions** screen — percentage, fixed-amount and free-delivery codes with a branch scope that is never guessed at, a tier scope, a minimum, dates, total and per-customer limits and first-order-only; valued on the server twice (cart, then again inside the checkout transaction with the coupon row locked), spent through `ECOM_COUPON_USAGE` whose unique index is the real limit, released when an order is cancelled, and landing on the invoice as an **exact** discount amount rather than a derived percentage. Automatic product offers were deliberately **not** duplicated — the ERP’s standing trade discount already does that job (`15 §5.1`). **Phase 10 (tracking and proof of delivery) is implemented**: V109 adds `ECOM_DELIVERY_TRACKING` and `ECOM_DELIVERY_PROOF`; a driver shares their position from the existing Delivery page while an order is out (only their own round, only while it is out, and a reading too vague to show anybody is refused), the customer sees how far away it is — a distance, never an invented ETA — and, afterwards, who took it and the photograph taken at the door; and `SaleStageChanged` now keeps the customer’s order in step with an invoice moved from Sales Activity or Delivery. **Phase 9 (delivery) is implemented**: V108 gives each pharmacy a delivery-charge policy (none / flat / base + per km, with an optional free-above threshold), one server-side service quotes it for the cart, the checkout screen and the invoice — re-quoting when the customer picks a different address, so the figure shown is the figure charged — the ERP books it to **Delivery Income** rather than revenue, and **Delivery › Tracking** gained the marketplace order number, the customer’s map pin and a tap-to-call number. **Phase 8 (the branch order board) is implemented**: V107 seeds **MOD53 · Sales › E-Commerce Orders** (`/ecomorders`) with rights mirroring Sales Activity, `/api/ecom/admin/orders` carries the branch-scoped board and its state machine — Confirm · Start packing · Mark ready · Dispatch · Delivered · Cancel, each mapped to the ERP stage the ERP already has (packing and ready deliberately move nothing) — and the ERP screen shows the lots to pick from, the delivery pin and the order’s history, while the customer’s own screen reads Placed → Confirmed → Packing → Ready → On the way → Delivered. **Phase 7 (POS integration polish) is implemented**: cancelling a sale finally works — `SalesServiceImpl.cancelSale` reverses the stock posting and the journals and marks the document (V106 `CANCELLED_BY` / `CANCELLED_DATE` / `CANCEL_REASON`, `STAGE=Cancelled`, `STATUS=N`), refusing once the goods are out or any money has been taken; a customer calling an online order off now cancels the pharmacy's invoice with it, in one transaction; every sales aggregate stops counting a cancelled sale; and Sales Activity gained a Cancelled chip, a Source filter, the `ECOM-…` order number beside the invoice number, and a cancel dialog. **Phase 6 (checkout) is implemented**: V105 `ECOM_ORDER` / `ECOM_ORDER_ITEM` / `ECOM_ORDER_STATUS_HISTORY`, `POST /api/ecom/orders` placing one cart as a real ERP invoice (`FROM_SOURCE=ECOM`) through `SalesServiceImpl.createSale(req, SaleContext)` in a single transaction, the customer app's Checkout / order confirmation / Orders screens, and a golden regression proving a staff sale still writes byte-identical MST, DTL, stock and ledger rows. The first concurrency test of it found a long-standing ERP defect — a lot could be oversold by two simultaneous sales — which is fixed and documented in `11 §6.2`. **Phase 5 (cart) is implemented**: V104 `ECOM_CART` / `ECOM_CART_ITEM` (one open cart per customer per pharmacy, no price stored), `/api/ecom/cart/**` priced and stock-checked on every read, and the Aura cart panel, badge, Add buttons and cart page in the customer app. **Phase 4 (price comparison) is implemented**: `GET /api/ecom/public/compare/{productId}` and the Aura ranked choice cards in the customer app. **Phase 3 (catalogue, live stock, tier pricing) is implemented**: `GET /api/ecom/public/catalogue[/{productId}]` — the pharmacies' own products priced by `SalesPriceCalculator` + `CustomerPricing` + `POS_SALES_DISCOUNT`, stock from `POS_STOCK_BATCH` (whole units per lot), guests quoted as retailers; Medicines, Product and the pharmacy shelf in the customer app. **Phase 2 (pharmacy discovery) is implemented**: V103 branch online-store columns, `GET /api/ecom/public/pharmacies[/{id}]` (nearest-first with Haversine, tenant-status gated), the Master Data › Branch form extended, and the Pharmacies map/list and pharmacy page in the customer app. **Phase 1 (global customer foundation) is implemented**: migrations V100–V102, the customer auth segment, registration/approval APIs, the shadow-customer bridge, the ERP console page *E-Commerce Customers* (MOD55), and the customer app as a separate project `../../friends-pharma-ecommerce` built on the Aura template.
