# Phase 14 — Gap Analysis

> Friends Pharma ERP · SRS v2.0 · 9 September 2026

12 gaps, each traced to code, schema or a live probe. Severity reflects business impact, not effort to fix.

| ID | Severity | Area | Current behaviour | Expected | Risk | Recommendation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GAP-01 | 🟢 Low | Access control | Collections and Sales Discount write endpoints were gated on ADMIN/MANAGER, authorities no ordinary user is granted. | The permission matrix decides. | Accounts and Sales Manager received 403 on four write endpoints. | Fixed 2026-09-06: checks moved into the services against MOD10 and MOD08. | RESOLVED |
| GAP-02 | 🟠 High | API surface | 31 endpoints under /api/v1/reports return HTTP 500. Written against columns this schema does not have. | No dead authenticated surface. | An integrator will find them in Swagger and trust them. | Delete the controller and its service, or gate it behind a disabled profile. The 18 grid reports under /api/reports are the live surface. | NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| GAP-03 | 🟠 High | Security | 8 of 25 accounts still store their password in clear text, having not signed in since the migration. | Every password a one-way hash. | A database copy is a copy of those passwords. | Reset them from the Passwords screen, which hashes immediately. They are already reported there as WEAK. | PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED |
| GAP-04 | 🟠 High | Payables | A purchase return reduces payable only when its payment type is ADJUSTMENT; returns inherit the purchase's type, so nearly none do. | Return credit reflected in what suppliers are owed. | Supplier outstanding is overstated wherever goods were returned. | A business decision is required: either every approved return against an invoice is credit, or the return screen must ask. | REQUIRES BUSINESS CONFIRMATION |
| GAP-05 | 🟡 Medium | Tenancy | The subscription gate exempts on isSuperAdmin() alone, without the organisation half of the platform-operator rule. | Only the platform operator bypasses the gate. | A customer's own administrator could pass the gate for their expired pharmacy. | Delegate to isPlatformOperator(), as ActingBranchFilter already does. | NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| GAP-06 | 🟡 Medium | Product history | POST /api/products/bulk updates in place; the single-product path versions. | One history rule whatever the route. | A price changed by import loses history the UI would have kept. | Route bulk edits through the version-creating method. | NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| GAP-07 | 🟡 Medium | Money rules | Two calculators cover the same arithmetic with different rounding: PurchaseCalculator divides at scale 10 then rounds; TransactionCalculator divides directly at scale 2. | One rounding policy. | A purchase and a sale of identical shape can differ by a paisa. | Fold the sales paths onto one calculator and delete the duplicate. | NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| GAP-08 | 🟡 Medium | Sales discounts | POS_SALES_DISCOUNT.PID_BRANCH is NOT NULL but the service never sets it, so creating a discount rule has never succeeded for anyone. The table holds 0 rows. | A usable discount feature. | A documented capability that has never worked. | Decide whether a rule is branch-scoped (set it from the session) or organisation-wide (make the column nullable), then one line. | REQUIRES BUSINESS CONFIRMATION |
| GAP-09 | 🟡 Medium | Configuration | app.platform.org-id is unset and defaults to 1. | Explicit per environment. | On an installation whose platform organisation is not 1, every platform-operator test silently fails. | Set it explicitly and fail startup if it names an organisation that does not exist. | NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| GAP-10 | 🟢 Low | Data integrity | Two stock movements reference transaction rows that no longer exist. | No orphan ledger rows. | Harmless to totals, fatal to an audit. | Add a foreign key with ON DELETE RESTRICT, then clean the two rows. | NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| GAP-11 | 🟢 Low | Test data | 19 purchase invoices with ZZAP/ZZDP challans from 26–27 August remain in the trading data. | Clean trading data. | They are counted by every purchase report and by supplier payable. | Remove them and their stock movements from non-production; confirm none reached production. | NOT IMPLEMENTED |
| GAP-12 | 🟢 Low | Audit | Purchase approval and cancellation change status but record no actor. | Attribution as password reset already has. | A disputed approval cannot be traced to a person. | Record actor and timestamp on the status change. | RECOMMENDED |
